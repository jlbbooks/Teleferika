# Device Fingerprint

Stable-ish device identifier used to bind a [[License Model|licence]] so it cannot be freely shared across phones.

Implemented in `device_fingerprint.dart` (uses `device_info_plus` et al.). Sent on validate/request to [[licence_server]].
